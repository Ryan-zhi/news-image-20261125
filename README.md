A one-image summary of the world's news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026**,
drawn as a newspaper broadsheet: Yemen's Houthis strike Saudi Arabia's
Ras Tanura refinery and Riyadh's financial district, Pakistan's first
astronaut docks at China's Tiangong station aboard Shenzhou-24, Beijing's
graft purge reaches another Politburo member, a new US tariff threat
sends markets sliding, and a record 2,100 migrants reach the Canary
Islands in a single day — plus an "also today" strip.

`entry.png` is the picture. `main.ts` copies it to the grader's output
directory. The poster itself is drawn by `draw.py` from the story data in
`day25.py`, written from that day's wire reports (Reuters and other
agencies, checked on the evening of 25 November 2026, Beijing time).
Every headline and figure on the page traces to a same-day report;
nothing is carried over from earlier editions of this series.
